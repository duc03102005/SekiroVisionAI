# Sekiro source research — 2026-09-10

The initial discovery catalog contained **60 distinct source URLs**: 54 YouTube videos, two Hugging Face datasets, two Internet Archive items, one Bilibili research demonstration and one historical Twitch VOD. The 54 YouTube titles and creator names were checked using the public oEmbed endpoint. Every requested target has at least one boss-specific candidate: **15 main bosses, three Inner bosses and 14 minibosses**. Main and Inner bosses each have at least two candidates. This is discovery coverage, not a downloaded or labeled training dataset.

**Real source videos acquired: 8 files, 40 minutes 9.855 seconds, 72,513 decoded frames.** This is raw recording duration including loading, menus and known excluded incidental desktop sections; it is not 40 minutes of labeled combat. [acquired_sources.json](acquired_sources.json) records verified media SHA256 values, actual PTS indices, the immutable distribution revision and complete rights/provenance chain. These are actual Sekiro gameplay frames, not the synthetic training fixtures. Frame-level attack/contact annotations are still unreviewed; no boss accuracy or all-boss coverage follows from acquisition alone.

The two longer sources are Markov's native Sekiro recordings, acquired through the [publisher-linked public dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/markov-ai/gaming-500-hours). Both complete MP4 files match the publisher's pinned LFS SHA256. Visual overview review confirmed Gyoubu Oniwa and Isshin the Sword Saint in one session; the other covers opening/tutorial traversal. [Permission evidence](permissions/markov-training-use.json) records the publisher's direct public training invitation, its context, dates and page hashes. This is training-use evidence, not a named open license or an original-media redistribution grant. No interaction logs were acquired and no source recordings are committed or redistributed.

Publisher descriptions were checked against decoded frames: the claim that all desktop content was removed is imperfect. A conservative exclusion covers 940–1060 seconds of the combat session and 0–260 seconds of the tutorial session. Only selected gameplay regions should feed mining/training until broader review. Source frames/timestamps remain unchanged; exclusions and coarse candidate intervals are recorded separately. These scene observations do not supply accepted attack/contact/TTI labels.

The smaller source is the [LIVE-YT-Gaming academic dataset](https://live.ece.utexas.edu/research/LIVE-YT-Gaming/index.html). Its publisher permits any-purpose use, copying, modification and distribution with its notice and attribution; the entire [notice](licenses/LIVE-YT-Gaming.txt) accompanies every local source. The [Q-Bench-Video authors](https://github.com/Q-Future/Q-Bench-Video) distribute a subset through their [public Hugging Face archive](https://huggingface.co/datasets/zhangzicheng/Q-Bench-Video); their [paper, Table 1 and Appendix 7](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.20063v2) identify the CG footage as LIVE-YT-Gaming. Six Sekiro entries were downloaded using ordinary HTTP Range requests, decoded, checked against ZIP entry CRC32/size and individually SHA256 hashed. The full 3.05 GB archive was not downloaded, so its advertised LFS hash is recorded separately from verified extracted-media hashes.

The six clips cover a torch/sword soldier fight and player death, tree/roof traversal, NPC dialogue, player attacks and an item pickup with fire effects. This supplies genuine motion and difficult negative context. Original player/session identities remain unknown, so all six are conservatively grouped together; they do not constitute six proven independent players. Temporal boundaries and observable contacts need explicit review before supervised attack/TTI training. The older findings below remain a record of reference-only sources, not a claim that data acquisition is still zero.

## How to read the files

- `video_sources.csv` has exactly the requested columns. Blank duration, resolution, FPS, upload date and phase mean unknown. A title containing “4K”, “60 FPS”, or “no damage” is a creator claim, not measured media metadata or a verified temporal annotation. Source types and boss names are discovery tags derived from titles/descriptions.
- Multiple bosses in one source use semicolon-separated names. The source keeps one stable ID and must remain in a single split. Do not duplicate a compilation under separate IDs to manufacture source diversity.
- `boss_coverage.csv` counts **acquired and reviewed** material in its numeric fields. Its notes separately give boss-specific discovered URL/creator counts. Boss rows remain `EMPTY` with score 0 until acquired material has reviewed boss labels. `UNREVIEWED` is a placeholder, not a claim that a fight has one phase. Replace it with reviewed phase rows after annotation.
- `boss_taxonomy.json` defines collection targets, common attack/state labels, negative tags and uncertainty rules. It does not assert a trained classifier's output order. Each versioned model must declare its actual trained head order and masks.

The live [YouTube terms](https://www.youtube.com/static?template=terms), particularly “Permissions and Restrictions”, distinguish watching/embedding from downloading or independent use. A creator's reuse offer does not by itself establish an authorized extraction route. [Creative Commons' Public Domain Mark](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/) describes a copyright-status assertion; it does not resolve an unrelated uploader's authority. These observations guide this catalog's status fields; they are not a claim that no usable Sekiro dataset can exist.

## Diversity and annotation priorities

Boss-specific candidates include Esoterickk, ONGBAL, THE GAMER EYES, Boss Fight Database, FuturePress, Ray Dhimitri, Kiet, Fuzzy Bearbarian, GamerGrade, TorpedoSoup and other distinct creators. The catalog includes normal fights, guides, playthroughs, no-hit/no-damage, low-attack-power and no-HUD challenges. This breadth is only a starting point: camera distance, lock-on, frame cadence, compression, arena, game patch, edits and repeated uploads require inspection after lawful acquisition. A guide may splice attacks or obscure frames with text; a no-HUD challenge may have a different recording pipeline. Do not equate creator count with independent players or sessions.

No-hit footage must preserve `impact_frame = null` when contact is not visible. A successful dodge or deflect does not create observed impact ground truth. Train TTI only under the annotation contract's evidence/mask rules. Keep perceived threat, observed player dodge and recommended safe dodge direction separate.

Review negative context alongside attacks: walking, idle, turning, player heals/attacks, enemy block/deflect/recovery, camera-only rotation/shake, particles/prosthetics, deathblows, cutscenes, nonattack jumps and feints. Do not automatically convert high model scores into hard-negative truth; queue them for review.

## Paths that can feed the implemented pipeline

1. Ingest the user's/app's own recordings with an explicit local recording ID and usage basis. Automatic recording must remain a visible user-controlled action in the Windows app.
2. Use creator-hosted downloads or openly licensed gameplay datasets once the actual grant and permitted access route are recorded. Preserve a source hash, acquisition date, creator/session/duplicate group and exact license evidence.
3. Where a catalog entry has only a viewing link or a scoped reuse offer, retain that entry as reference material. No messages were sent to creators and no paid access was purchased during this task.
4. Mine short candidates with their original timestamps, review boundaries and uncertainty, then create source/player/session-disjoint splits. Add held-out-boss evaluation when acquired data supports it. Train a baseline as soon as a small real set is available; completeness of this 32-target collection is not a prerequisite.

Actual footage is now available for training and replay development, but this small initial set does not establish a trained multi-boss model or live Sekiro accuracy. Synthetic smoke data remains separately marked and excluded from gameplay dataset/model claims. Training results, supported heads, held-out performance and release eligibility must be reported from their own evidence.
